## 👨‍💻 About Me

Android Developer & NLP Researcher passionate about building technology that creates real-world impact. I work at the intersection of **computational psychiatry**, **LLM safety**, and **mobile development** — from designing clinically explainable suicide-risk detection frameworks to building production-grade Android apps with MVVM + Jetpack Compose.

Currently pursuing my BSc in Software Engineering (Data Science major) at Daffodil International University, with three research papers under review at PLOS Digital Health, PLOS ONE, and Astronomy & Computing.
